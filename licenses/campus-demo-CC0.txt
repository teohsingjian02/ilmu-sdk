Campus Guide model and motion library

The Campus Guide model, its procedural geometry, materials, rig, face shapes,
and six body animations were newly authored for this ILMU project using
tools/make-campus-demo.py. They contain no imported models, textures,
motion capture, Mixamo clips or Business Lady content.

To the extent possible under law, the author dedicates these original demo
assets to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal. You may use, modify and
redistribute them, including as downloadable GLB files, without attribution.

Full dedication and fallback licence:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en

The included greeting is generic Malay speech generated using the project's
OpenAI and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech accounts. It contains no personal data
or third-party recording. Its text and timing are provided for demonstration;
the service terms of those providers apply to generated speech.

MMU's name and any university marks are not covered by this dedication.

SDK source and third-party dependencies retain their existing licences.
